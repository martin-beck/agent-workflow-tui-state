# AR-0034: Rich synthetic scenario inputs

Extend the synthetic TUI corpus beyond control-key coverage with semantically
distinct, privacy-safe inputs that exercise complete decision-session paths.
Every fixture must carry a valid revision-bound context, a bounded action trace,
and an explicit use-case category. Validation must reject malformed identity,
digest, revision, action, or expected-event data before replay.

The product change is intentionally limited to the corpus and its validator;
the scenario runner, generated documentation, and CI workflow consume the
same corpus and remain owned by their respective ARs.
