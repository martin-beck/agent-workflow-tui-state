# AR-0035: Deterministic scenario artifact hardening

Harden the scenario replay and screenshot generator so invalid actions and
unsafe artifact identifiers fail before writing, and freshness checks detect
both missing and stale generated files. Generated SVGs must carry deterministic
screen-reader metadata while retaining the existing privacy-safe visual trace.

The product checkout owns `tools/run_scenarios.py`, its runner tests, and the
generated screenshots. CI and generated Markdown remain owned by AR-0031 and
AR-0032 respectively.
