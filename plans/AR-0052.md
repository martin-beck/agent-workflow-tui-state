# AR-0052: Modal own-proposal editing and guarded exit

Provide four single-line fields for label, rationale, confidence, and
trade-offs. Tab, Enter, and Up/Down move between fields while action keys are
entered as text. Require an explicit arrow-selectable Yes/No confirmation before
adding the proposal. Show selected, clarification, remaining, and saved counts;
when quitting with missing work, list the exact missing decisions and save
requirement and ask whether to remain in the TUI.
