# AR-0061: Cross-platform remote-agent round trips

Exercise complete deterministic round trips from Linux and Windows clients,
through the reviewed SSH/PowerShell transport, to a remote agent and back
through the Coordinator persistence boundary. Cover TUI and GUI initiation,
capability negotiation, launch-pending/attached/awaiting-response/detached
states, reconnect, stale and duplicate responses, clarification, user-owned
proposals, and final AR revision updates.

Acceptance criteria:

- Linux-to-remote and Windows-to-remote fixtures both complete a valid
  revision-bound request/response round trip.
- Any host, project, AR revision, request, session, sequence, or capability
  mismatch fails closed without mutating the AR.
- Network failure, timeout, detached UI, remote-agent failure, and reconnect
  preserve an explicit awaiting state and do not authorize autonomous work.
- CI runs the full fixture offline or against an isolated ephemeral SSH
  service, with redacted logs and reproducible diagnostics.
